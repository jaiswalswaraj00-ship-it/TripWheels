Frequently Asked Questions

How can I book a vehicle?

Go to Available Vehicles, select a vehicle, choose your rental date and number of days, then confirm your booking.

Where can I see my booking?

Open the My Bookings section from your dashboard to view your bookings and their status.

What should I do if my booking is not visible?

Refresh the My Bookings page and check your booking details. If the problem continues, contact TripWheels support.

How can I report a vehicle problem?

Contact support using the phone, email or WhatsApp options above and provide your booking details.

⚠️

Safety First

In case of an immediate emergency or danger, contact your local emergency services first. TripWheels support is for platform, booking and rental assistance.
